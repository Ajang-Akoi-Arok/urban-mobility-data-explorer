# Report on CodeVibe Tracking Inconsistencies

**To:** Sir Hubert
**Subject:** Report on CodeVibe Reports and Our Completed Assignment
**Date:** 15 October 2026

## 1. Introduction

Our group would like to raise concerns about the inconsistencies we have experienced with CodeVibe while working on the assignment. We have completed the assignment and submitted our CodeVibe reports as required. However, the results shown in the reports do not always reflect our actual coding process and the work we have put into the assignment.

Throughout the project, we have been manually writing our code, working on our files, and collaborating through Git. Despite these efforts, CodeVibe has reported AI usage levels that do not appear to match our experience.

## 2. Issues Experienced

### 2.1 AI Usage Reports Do Not Reflect Our Actual Work

Our main concern is that CodeVibe sometimes reports high AI usage even when we have been manually typing and writing our code. This creates a gap between the work we have actually done and what the reports indicate.

### 2.2 Significant Changes After Git Pull

We have also observed that pulling code from other contributors using `git pull` can coincide with a significant increase in the reported AI usage percentage. For example, one group member reported an increase from 17% to 98% after running `git pull`.

This raises concerns about how CodeVibe tracks code changes and attributes activity in collaborative projects.

### 2.3 Other Tracking Inconsistencies

We have also experienced unexpected Agent Mode and inline autocomplete detections. In addition, some group members have reported that the CodeVibe dashboard displays no data, making it difficult to monitor their coding activity.

## 3. Impact on Our Group

Our group has completed the assignment and submitted the required reports. We have made an effort to write our code manually, collaborate on the project, and track our progress using CodeVibe.

However, the inconsistencies in the reports have caused concern because the reported AI usage does not always seem to correspond with our actual coding process. We are concerned that relying only on these percentages may not provide a complete picture of our individual contributions and the work completed by the group.

## 4. Request for Review

We kindly request that our completed assignment and submitted CodeVibe reports be reviewed together, taking into consideration the actual code, our Git history, and other available evidence of our development process.

We would also appreciate further clarification on how CodeVibe handles manually written code, changes introduced through `git pull`, and other activities that may affect the reported AI usage.

## 5. Conclusion

We are submitting this report to bring these issues to your attention and explain the challenges we have experienced with CodeVibe. We have completed the assignment, submitted our reports, and made an effort to document our work throughout the process.

We remain committed to following the assignment requirements and using CodeVibe to track our progress. We simply want to ensure that our work is reviewed fairly and that the inconsistencies in the reports are taken into account during the assessment.

Thank you for your time, understanding, and consideration.
